# High-Value Metal Extraction Using Microorganisms  

## Overview  
The world faces pressing environmental challenges such as **inefficient waste management, poor recycling practices, and unsustainable resource extraction**.  
A major concern is **e-waste and mining residues**, which release toxins into the environment while wasting valuable metals.  

This project addresses these issues by exploring the **eco-friendly extraction of high-value metals** from e-waste and ores using microorganisms. Specifically, we leverage the bioleaching properties of **Chromobacterium violaceum**, supported by a **custom-designed bioreactor**, to efficiently recover metals such as **gold, platinum, and copper** while minimizing environmental harm.  

---

## Objectives  
- **Tackle sustainability challenges** by providing a greener alternative to traditional mining and recycling.  
- **Utilize microorganisms** for efficient and eco-friendly metal extraction.  
- **Develop a custom bioreactor** tailored to support bioleaching under optimal microbial growth conditions.  

---

## Features  
- **Microbial Metal Extraction**: Leverages *Chromobacterium violaceum* for sustainable bioleaching of precious metals.  
- **Custom Bioreactor**: Engineered to maintain ideal pH, temperature, aeration, and agitation for microbial activity.  
- **Eco-friendly Solution**: Reduces reliance on toxic chemical leaching and energy-intensive smelting processes.  
- **Circular Economy Approach**: Turns hazardous e-waste into a valuable resource stream, reducing landfill and pollution.  

---

## Bioreactor Design  
Our bioreactor is specifically designed to:  
- Maintain **optimal pH levels** for microbial activity.  
- Ensure **temperature control** within a range favorable to *Chromobacterium violaceum*.  
- Provide **aeration and agitation** for even nutrient distribution.  
- Include a **metal recovery chamber** for efficient collection of bioleached metals.
